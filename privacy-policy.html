Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Who We Are

VisionLab AI is a business run by Stephen Rivas in London, Ontario. We build AI phone receptionists, automations and websites for service businesses.

Stephen is responsible for how VisionLab AI handles personal information. You can reach him at stephen@visionlabai.ca.

The Short Version

We collect what you give us when you book a call, call our AI line, try the demo on this site or pay us. We use it to talk with you and run our service. Calls with our AI assistant are recorded. We don't sell your information.

What We Collect

  • When you book a call: your name, email, phone number if you give it, and anything you write in the booking notes.
  • When you call our AI line, (365) 360-4080, or use "Talk to our AI" on this site: the audio of the call, a written transcript and summary, your phone number (caller ID) on phone calls, and anything you tell the assistant, such as your name, email, phone number and business. The website demo uses your microphone only after you start a call.
  • When you pay us: Stripe collects your card details. We see your name, email and the amount, never your full card number.
  • When you email us: your email address and your message.

This website has no contact forms, accounts or analytics, and it doesn't set cookies.

Calls Are Recorded

Calls with our AI assistant are recorded and transcribed. We use the recordings to log what you asked for, book or change appointments, and check how well the assistant handled the call. If you'd rather not be recorded, hang up and email us instead.

How We Use Your Information

  • To answer your questions and follow up on what you asked about
  • To book, change or cancel your call with us, and send confirmations and reminders
  • To call you back when you ask for a callback
  • To review and improve how our AI assistant handles calls
  • To send receipts and invoices
  • To follow up with you later about our services. Tell us anytime if you'd like us to stop.

We don't sell your information, and we won't add you to a newsletter or mailing list without your consent.

Services That Handle It For Us

We use these services to run VisionLab AI. Each one only gets what it needs to do its job:

  • Retell AI runs the AI assistant and records and transcribes calls. To understand and answer callers, it uses AI model providers, such as OpenAI for language and ElevenLabs for voice.
  • Twilio provides the phone line.
  • Cal.com handles bookings and confirmation emails (cal.com/privacy).
  • n8n moves information between these tools automatically.
  • ClickUp is where we keep call logs and booking records.
  • Zoho Mail handles our email.
  • Stripe processes payments.
  • esm.sh, Google Fonts and Tailwind deliver code and fonts for our pages. Like any website request, they receive your IP address.

Most of these services are based in the United States, so your information may be stored and processed there and be subject to US law.

When We Work For a Client Business

If you call a business that uses a system we built, your information belongs to that business. We handle it on their behalf, only to run the service they hired us for, under a written agreement. We never use it for our own marketing.

For health clinics, the clinic stays responsible for your health information under Ontario's Personal Health Information Protection Act, or the health privacy law in its province, and we act as its service provider. Questions about that information should go to the business you called, and we'll help them answer.

How Long We Keep It

We keep your contact details and notes from our conversations so we can follow up with you later. We keep call recordings and transcripts only as long as we need them to provide our service.

If you ask, we'll stop contacting you and delete your information, unless the law requires us to keep it. Payment records, for example, are kept for tax purposes.

Keeping It Safe

Only Stephen and the services listed above can access your information, and we use the security features those services provide. If a breach ever puts your information at real risk, we'll tell you and report it as the law requires.

Your Rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. You can also withdraw your consent at any time. Email stephen@visionlabai.ca and we'll reply within 30 days.

If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
